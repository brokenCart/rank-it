How to Play

  1. 1

    Gather

    Create a room and invite your friends with the code.

  2. 2

    Draw

    Everyone gets the same prompt and draws their best masterpiece.

  3. 3

    Rank

    Rank everyone else's drawings from best to worst. No voting for yourself!

  4. 4

    Win

    The rankings are added up and the top artist is crowned the winner.
